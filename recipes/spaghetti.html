Spaghetti

Description

A classic Italian dish made with al dente spaghetti, rich tomato sauce, and a touch of garlic and herbs.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 1 cup marinara sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions.
  2. In a pan, heat olive oil and sauté minced garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add marinara sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Toss the spaghetti in the sauce until well coated.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, and top with Parmesan cheese.
