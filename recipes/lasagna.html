Lasagna

Description

This classic lasagna recipe is a comforting dish made with layers of rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and gooey mozzarella. It's perfect for family dinners or special occasions and can be made ahead of time.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 pound Italian sausage
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a large skillet, heat olive oil and cook ground beef and Italian sausage until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. Add marinara sauce to the meat mixture and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a bowl, mix ricotta cheese, eggs, Parmesan cheese, salt, and basil.
  6. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a baking dish.
  7. Layer lasagna noodles, ricotta mixture, mozzarella cheese, and meat sauce. Repeat layers, ending with mozzarella on top.
  8. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  9. Remove foil and bake for an additional 10 minutes, until cheese is bubbly and golden.
  10. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before serving.
